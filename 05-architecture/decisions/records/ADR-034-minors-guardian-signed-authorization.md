# ADR-034 — Minors are enabled only with a registered guardian, accepted terms and a signed authorization on file

- **ID:** ADR-034
- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Authors:** FaceAttend EDU Team
- **Related:** ADR-009 (MinIO; this ADR adds identity as a second user of it), ADR-026, `FULL/docs/MENORES_ACUDIENTES_Y_TERMINOS.md`; `FULL` PR #309.

---

## Context

Many learners hold a TI (under 18). Law 1581/2012 art. 7 and Decree 1074/2015 require the authorization of the legal representative to process a minor's data, and biometric data is sensitive. The system already registered the guardian and recorded the terms accepted in person, and the biometric service refused minors without them; but no evidence of the signed authorization was kept (finding L-01, critical).

---

## Decision

**We decided:** a minor is enabled for biometric features only when, in this order, (1) a guardian is registered (adult, CC/CE/PP, contact required), (2) the guardian's **signed authorization** is on file, and (3) the guardian accepted the terms in person before a school administrator.

1. A person is a minor by birth date; without a birth date, a TI is treated as a minor.
2. The enablement (`GET /persons/{id}/terms`) reports a `pendingStep` of `GUARDIAN`, `DOCUMENT` or `TERMS`; the biometric service refuses with `PERSON_NOT_ENABLED` until it is `NONE`.
3. The administrator uploads the signed file (`POST /persons/{id}/guardian/document`): PDF, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB, type read from the bytes, object key generated by the server. **Identity stores it in MinIO** (bucket `guardian-documents`, SSE-S3) and keeps location and metadata in five `guardian.consent_document_*` columns (migration `id-018`). Only administrators download it (`attachment`, `nosniff`).
4. Replacing the guardian with a different person requires a new document and new terms.
5. `faceattend.guardian.document-required=false` switches the document step off to stage a rollout.

**Justification:** the legal requirement is evidence, not a checkbox; MinIO is already the evidence store for justification supports (ADR-009), so reusing it keeps one encrypted object store.

---

## Evaluated alternatives

| Alternative | Pros | Cons | Reason for discarding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signed file in MinIO, metadata in `identity.guardian` — chosen | Real evidence, encrypted, same store as supports | Identity gains a MinIO dependency | — (chosen) |
| Guardian data only (no file) | Simplest | Weak evidence of the signature | Chosen against by the product owner |
| File as `bytea` in PostgreSQL | No new dependency | Database growth; backups heavier | MinIO already exists for this |
| Store it in attendance's supporting-document service | Reuse | Cross-context coupling for identity data | Breaks context ownership (ADR-002) |

---

## Consequences

**Positive:** closes L-01 in code; the evidence can be produced on request.

**Negative / Trade-offs:**
- On deploy, minors whose guardian was registered before this change move to `DOCUMENT_PENDING` until a file is uploaded (schools must be warned, or deploy with the switch off).
- The migration must run before identity starts in production (`ddl-auto: validate`).
- Apps that do not know the `DOCUMENT` step show it as `TERMS` (degrades, does not break).

**Impact on the system:** `01-ms-identity`, `99-api-gateway` (6 MB limit on that route only), Web (`MinorEnrollmentModal`), Mobile (blocked-step message), DBML.

---

## Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| The authorization form does not meet the legal wording | Medium | High | Legal must approve the form (open item) |
| MinIO unavailable during upload | Low | Low | 503 and no orphan objects (object removed if the row cannot be saved) |
